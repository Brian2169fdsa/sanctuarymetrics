Method & assumptions

  • Sources: Admin-center exports of Active users, Product list, Subscriptions (both billing profiles), invoice lists and seven invoice PDFs (Jul–Sep 2026) for unit pricing, Microsoft 365 active user detail, Mailbox usage, OneDrive usage, SharePoint site usage, Teams user activity and Teams team activity, all at the 180-day period, refreshed 2026-09-27.
  • "Idle" = days since the most recent activity date across Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams. Accounts with no activity date in 180 days are treated as never active.
  • Savings are stated per month at the prices shown. A seat on an annual term (Business Basic, 43 Standard seats, Copilot, Entra P2) is reclaimable — it avoids a future purchase — but does not lower the invoice until its renewal date. Seats renewing in October 2026 are cash reductions.
  • Standard→Basic candidates are Standard holders with no desktop Word/Excel/PowerPoint activity in the Microsoft 365 Apps usage report for 180 days.
  • Business Premium is treated as a security control, not a cost line. Cutting it is a compliance decision.
  • Also used: Microsoft 365 Apps usage (desktop vs web/mobile), Office activations, Email activity, OneDrive and SharePoint activity, Teams device usage, shared mailbox and guest list. Not exported: Intune device enrollment, third-party enterprise apps.
